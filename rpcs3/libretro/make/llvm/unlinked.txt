# Demangle and the execution engine is kept whether pulled or not, since
# that is where what differs by system is. A link that asks for one of
# these says which: take its line out of here and out of llvm.mk comes back
# with gen.py - or add the file to llvm.mk's list by hand.
#
# Also here, whatever the directory: files that had locks in them and that
# nothing refers to since the cuts in LLVM's sources (CHANGELOG) - the
# DWARF and profile readers, crash recovery, the thread pool and the like.
